How it works

Play today. Ask for something. Wake up to a new game.

01

Play

Today's version is always one tap away. It takes about ten seconds to learn and much longer to master.

02

Ask

Suggest one change in plain words, like “give my tail teeth.” The top requests go to a vote.

03

It evolves overnight

At midnight the winning change gets built, tested against the house rules, and shipped. Exactly one change, every night.

Questions

Won't it turn into a mess?

Won't the crowd ruin it?Maybe on one branch. But it changes one step at a time, every earlier version survives, and anyone can fork from a good day. Bad ideas lose players, and good branches keep them.
Who actually builds the change?An AI builds it overnight from the winning request. Then a check confirms the game still runs and the change matches what people voted for. If it fails, the runner-up ships.
Why keep every version?Because the history is the fun part. You can play the night a feature was born, see who asked for it, and pick up a branch the main line left behind.
Is Orbit real?The game on this page is a working prototype. Every version in the tree runs, and you can play them all. The players, votes, posts and prices are made up to show the idea.
