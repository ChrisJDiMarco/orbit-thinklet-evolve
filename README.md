# Orbit (Thinklet Evolve concept)

A concept landing page for "Thinklet Evolve", built around Orbit, a small arcade game that is never finished. The pitch: every night the game ships one change its players voted for, and every earlier version stays playable forever.

The game on the page is a working prototype drawn on a canvas. A comet circles a ring; tap to reverse direction, hold to drop to the inner ring, and catch sparks while dodging rocks. It runs on autopilot until you click to take over.

The page includes a family tree of 14 playable versions across three branches, and clicking any node plays that version exactly as it was:

- **Main line, days 1 to 9:** sparks, drifting rocks, a second ring, splitting sparks, speed-up, a solid tail that smashes rocks, a twin comet, a magnet, and meteor showers (the live Day 9 build).
- **Zen fork (from day 3):** no rocks and no score, then light ripples on each catch.
- **Blackout fork (from day 5):** darkness, sonar pings, and glowing sparks.

The rest of the page is concept material: how it works, a Day 10 ballot with suggestions, mock social posts, an FAQ and a "Follow Orbit" email form. The players, votes, posts and prices are invented to show the idea. Votes, suggestions and the email form stay in the page; nothing is sent or saved.

Made by Chris DiMarco with an AI advisor (the Chief of Staff desk in Cabinet), September 2026.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It is one self-contained file with no external scripts, fonts or images, and no build step.
